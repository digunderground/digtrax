// what to evaluate

  • density — does the tighter row layout (vs V1's tile grid) feel more efficient or just cramped?
  • single-typeface — does mono-only read as "power tool" or "I can't read this for 4 hours"?
  • no decoration — do hard rectangles + 1px borders feel honest or cold?
  • phosphor green accent — iconic terminal color. Brand-pairing concern: replaces OneTagger's teal.
  • ASCII waveform — pulls the brutalist thesis through to the player. Does it feel coherent or affected?
