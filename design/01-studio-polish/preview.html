What to evaluate

  • Quick Tag — does the sidebar + track grid + inspector + player layout read as the priority feature?
  • Tag Editor bulk pane — do "apply to all" toggles + multi-value states make the Phase 2 bulk-edit flow obvious?
  • Settings as a route — does the macOS-style category sidebar feel right vs the current modal dialog?
  • Light vs Dark — toggle in the header. Both should look polished, not invert-flipped.
  • Density — too tight? too airy? Body is 14px (smaller than typical) — does it work for a tool that's meant to be looked at for hours?
