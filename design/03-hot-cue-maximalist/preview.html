What to evaluate

  • Glow halos on selected tiles — track tiles glow in their primary tag color when selected. Does this read as energy/identity or as visual noise?
  • Translucent vibrancy panels — sidebar, inspector, player bar all use backdrop-filter blur. On macOS this looks like Logic Pro. On older Linux it falls back to solid (graceful).
  • Space Grotesk for display — wide grotesque, late-2010s techno-magazine feel. Pairs with the energetic aesthetic.
  • 3-font system — Dosis (brand only), Space Grotesk (display), Geist (body), JetBrains Mono (data). Is variation an asset or distraction?
  • Player bar 112px tall — taller than V1's 96px to give the waveform room. Performance-tool DNA — but takes vertical space from the track grid.
  • Cool navy base — `#050811` instead of neutral black. Makes the saturated track colors pop more. Does the slight blue tint feel right or off-brand?
